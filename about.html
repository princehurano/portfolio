About Me

Personal Introduction

Hi, I'm Prince Darwin A. Hurano, a Grade 12 student taking up the TVL-ICT Strand. I enjoy working with computers and learning how technology can be used to solve everyday problems. This page shares a bit more about my background, interests, and where I hope to go after senior high school.

Short Biography

I was born and raised in the Philippines, where I have always been curious about how things work. Growing up, I enjoyed tinkering with gadgets and exploring computers, which eventually led me to choose the ICT/TVL Strand in senior high school. Through this strand, I have learned the basics of computer systems, programming, and web development. Outside of academics, I like spending time on hobbies that help me relax and recharge. I believe in continuous learning and try to pick up a new skill whenever I get the chance. My goal is to keep improving both my technical and personal skills so I can contribute meaningfully to my future workplace and community.
